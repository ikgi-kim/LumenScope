LumenScope Privacy Policy

Updated November 24, 2026

This policy takes effect when published. It describes how LumenScope, an Android app for viewing and analyzing IES photometric files, handles information. LumenScope is operated by IK-GI Kim.

1. Files, photos, and permissions

IES files and PDF reports. LumenScope reads the IES files you select in the app or open from another app. Photometric calculations run on your device. A PDF report contains the file information and analysis sections you choose, and is written to the destination you select. LumenScope does not upload IES files, photometric results, or PDF reports to the developer, Firebase, or AdMob. If you select a cloud storage provider as your PDF destination, the provider handles the file under its own terms.

Camera and site photos. LumenScope requests camera permission when you choose Take Photo. Site photos are stored in the app’s storage on your device and associated with the open IES file by filename. The app does not upload site photos. You can decline or revoke camera access in Android settings and continue using the analysis and PDF features.

File access. LumenScope uses the Android system file picker or the access another app grants for the selected file. It uses the Android save dialog for PDF export.

2. Firebase and advertising

Feature configuration. After the first-run privacy screen, Firebase Remote Config checks feature availability, such as whether the 3D viewer is available. Google may process an installation identifier, app and system information, and technical request data to deliver configuration. This configuration check is separate from the optional data settings below.

Usage analytics. If you enable Usage analytics, Google Analytics for Firebase receives app activity such as app sessions, analysis screens opened, and whether an IES file opened or a PDF export succeeded, failed, or was canceled. The SDK may also process app and device information, an app-instance identifier, and approximate location derived from network information. We use this data to understand which features are used and where users encounter difficulties. Custom usage events do not include file names, paths, or file contents.

Crash and performance reports. If you enable Crash and performance reports, Firebase Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring may receive crash details, stack traces, installation identifiers, app and device metadata, and processing times for tasks such as opening an IES file, generating a 3D mesh, or creating a PDF. We use this information to diagnose errors and improve reliability and speed. If both analytics and diagnostics are enabled, Crashlytics may include recent Analytics events to help explain a crash.

Banner ads and ad choices. If you enable Banner ads, LumenScope uses Google AdMob and checks Google’s User Messaging Platform before requesting ads. These services may process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers where available, ad interactions, diagnostic information, and consent status to deliver or measure ads, prevent invalid activity, and respect privacy choices. Ads may be personalized where the applicable settings and consent allow it.

3. Your choices

Usage analytics, crash and performance reports, and banner ads are off before you make your choices. You can use IES analysis and PDF export with all three disabled. To change your choices later, open Settings → Privacy & Permissions. When Google requires a separate ad privacy options form, it is available under Settings → Ad Privacy Choices. Changing a choice affects future collection and does not automatically remove previously processed data.

4. Storage, retention, and deletion

App-stored photos and privacy preferences generally remain in app storage until overwritten, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Original IES files, exported PDFs, and any older photos in shared storage may remain after uninstalling. Delete those copies in the file manager or storage service where you saved them. Android or your chosen storage provider may retain backups or other copies under its own settings.

Service data processed by Google is retained according to the applicable Google service settings and retention practices. To request access, correction, or deletion of personal data, or to ask about other rights available under applicable law, contact us using the email below.

5. Service providers and international processing

Google processes the service data described above through Firebase, Google Analytics, AdMob, and User Messaging Platform. Google may process data in the United States and other countries where it operates infrastructure. For information on Google’s handling of data from apps that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, the Google Privacy Policy, and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

6. Children’s privacy

LumenScope is designed for photometric analysis and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us so we can review the request.

7. Contact and policy changes

For privacy questions or requests, contact IK-GI Kim at skyfind@kakao.com. We update this policy when our practices change. The revision date at the top identifies the current version.
